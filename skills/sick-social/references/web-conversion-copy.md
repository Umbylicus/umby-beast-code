# Website Conversion Copy & Product Microcopy Playbook

Every word on a flagship website (Apple, Stripe, Linear, Shopify, Aesop) does a specific job. Apply these rules whenever `beast-code` generates or upgrades landing pages, storefronts, or application UI.

---

## 1. Hero Headlines & Subheads

- **Mechanism + Outcome, Zero Hype Words:**
  - *Slop:* `"Supercharge Your Financial Workflow With Next-Gen AI Automation"`
  - *Stripe/Mercury Caliber:* `"Close your books every night, not ten days after quarter-end."`
  - *Apple/Hardware Caliber:* `"Studio acoustics. Pocket weight."` *(Use short fragments **only** for deliberate hardware lockups, never repeated across body copy.)*
- **Subhead Rule (`15–28` words max):** Explain *how* the headline happens in literal domain nouns: *"Connect Ramp, Stripe, and NetSuite in four minutes. Every payout reconciles automatically against your chart of accounts with a full audit trail."*

## 2. CTAs & Button Labels

- **Verb + Concrete Object:**
  - Replace `"Submit"`, `"Get Started"`, `"Learn More"`, `"Click Here"` with `"Start interactive sandbox"`, `"Create free workspace"`, `"Add 250g bag · $24"`, `"Read security architecture"`.
- **Risk-Reducing Microcopy Under Primary CTAs:** When asking for signup or purchase, state concrete facts in `12px–13px` muted ink directly beneath: `"No credit card required · SOC2 Type II"` or `"Ships today from Austin · Free 30-day returns"`. (Only state facts that are true for this product!)

## 3. UI States: Empty, Loading & Error Microcopy

- **Errors Name Cause + Exit Path:**
  - *Bad:* `"Invalid input."` / `"Something went wrong (Error 500)."`
  - *Good:* `"Card declined by your bank for insufficient funds. Try another card or pay via ACH."` / `"Could not reach Stripe. Your draft invoice is saved locally; retry in a moment."`
- **Empty States Invite Action:**
  - *Bad:* `"No webhooks found."`
  - *Good:* `"No webhook endpoints yet. Add an endpoint URL to receive live order events."` + `[ Add endpoint ]`
