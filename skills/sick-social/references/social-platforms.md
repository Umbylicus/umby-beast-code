# Platform-Native Social Media Playbooks (Zero Cringe, High Signal)

## 1. X / Twitter (Single Posts & Long-Form Posts)

- **Prefer Long-Form Single Posts Over Numbered `1/12` Threads:** Modern X rewards tight, self-contained 80-to-220-word single posts (or crisp 3–5 post chains when showing multiple images/snippets). Never write `"🧵 Thread:"` or `"1/"`.
- **The First Line Carries Concrete Information, Not Clickbait:**
  - *Slop Hook:* `"99% of startups fail at pricing. Here are 7 secrets I learned scaling to $10M ARR 👇"`
  - *Human Operator Hook:* `"We raised our entry plan from $19 to $79 last month expecting churn to spike. Support tickets dropped 62% and net new revenue went up."`
- **Visual Pairing:** Whenever posting technical, design, or product insights, suggest the exact raw artifact to attach (a cropped terminal benchmark, a before/after UI diff, a rough napkin state diagram—never an AI-generated DALL-E neon robot image).
- **Cadence:** Group 2–3 sentences into real paragraphs separated by whitespace; reserve standalone 1-line breaks for a single earned turn.

## 2. LinkedIn (High-Status Operator & Founder Voice)

LinkedIn is flooded with "broetry" (one dramatic 6-word sentence per double-spaced line ending in *"Agree?"*). Stand out immediately by writing like a calm, experienced executive or principal engineer sharing field notes:
- **Paragraph Shape:** Real 2-to-4-sentence paragraphs. Zero emoji bullet lists (`✅`, `🚀`, `🔥`).
- **Opening 2 Lines (Before `"…see more"`):** State the concrete observation, experiment result, or hiring/product lesson plainly in line 1–2 without bait-and-switch drama.
- **Close:** End with a specific operational takeaway or a genuine domain question peers actually have opinions on (*"For teams running multi-region Postgres, where did read-replica lag bite you first?"*—never *"Thoughts? 👇"*).

## 3. Hacker News (`Show HN`), Reddit & Technical Communities

- **Zero Marketing Adjectives:** Technical communities punish hype instantly. Strip every evaluative word (*fast, modern, intuitive, next-gen, powerful*).
- **Lead With Architecture, Stack & Tradeoffs:**
  - State what it does in one literal sentence, how it works under the hood, why existing tools didn't fit, and **what current limitations or rough edges exist today**. Admitting real tradeoffs builds 10x more trust than claiming perfection.

## 4. Short-Form Video / Carousel Hooks (Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, IG Carousels)

- **Spoken Breath Test:** Every sentence must be speakable in one natural breath (`<=16` words), using contractions (`we're`, `didn't`, `it's`, `won't`).
- **0-to-3 Second Visual + Verbal Hook:** Open by showing the finished result or broken failure on screen first (*"Look at why this checkout button loses half its mobile taps"*) before explaining how to fix it.

## 5. Local Business Platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Google Business Profile)

Most local clients (restaurants, garages, contractors, salons) reach customers here, not on X or Hacker News. Write as the business talking to neighbors: plain, specific, and local. Name the real street, the real dish, the real job that came in this week. The founder-and-engineer voice in sections 1 to 3 does not fit these accounts.

### Facebook (Business Page)
- **First Line Does the Work:** Only the first couple of lines show before "See more". Put the news there (*"The brake lathe is back up, so same-day rotor jobs are open again starting Monday."*).
- **Length:** 40 to 120 words. Real sentences in one or two short paragraphs.
- **Links & Hashtags:** One link at most, placed after the copy. Skip hashtags or use one local tag.
- **Photo:** Suggest a real photo from the shop, kitchen, or job site. Never stock or AI-generated images of people or work the business did not do.

### Instagram (Feed Post or Reel Caption)
- **Caption Cutoff:** Roughly the first 125 characters show before "more". Lead with the hook there.
- **Links:** Links in captions are not clickable. Point to the link in bio, or use a CTA the reader can act on without a link (call, DM, stop by).
- **Hashtags:** 3 to 5 specific tags at the end (city, neighborhood, trade). No walls of 30 tags.
- **Photo First:** The image carries the post. Say which real photo or clip the caption is written for.

### Google Business Profile (Updates, Offers, Events)
- **Length:** The limit is 1,500 characters, but only the opening shows in Search and Maps. Aim for 80 to 300 characters and put the point in the first sentence.
- **Use the Button as the CTA:** Pick the one button that matches the goal (`Book`, `Order online`, `Buy`, `Learn more`, `Sign up`, or `Call now`). Do not repeat a second, different CTA in the text.
- **No Phone Numbers in the Text:** Google can reject posts with a phone number in the body. Use the `Call now` button instead.
- **No Hashtags:** They do nothing on Google and read as spam.
- **Search Wording, Naturally:** Mention the service and the town once, the way a person would say it. Never stack keywords or list nearby cities.
- **Offer Posts:** An Offer post needs real terms and dates. Write one only when the owner has approved the exact offer.
- **Reviews:** Asking happy customers for a review is fine. Never offer anything in exchange for a review, and never write or quote a review that is not real.
